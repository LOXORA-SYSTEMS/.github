# Code of Conduct

We want LOXORA SYSTEMS to be a respectful, professional space for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code reviews.
- Give and accept feedback with a focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect confidentiality of clients and colleagues.
- Be honest about mistakes and deadlines.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks
- Sharing private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report concerns to solomboni5@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately, and violations may lead to removal from the organization or its projects.
